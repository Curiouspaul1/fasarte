Open walls

Exhibit at Artestoa

Every empty frame in the hall is waiting for a maker. If you make things by hand (beadwork, textiles, ceramics, wood, leather or metal), your work can hang here, in a gallery of its own.

  1. I

    Photograph each piece

    On a plain backdrop, in soft daylight. A few angles of each piece is perfect.

  2. II

    One folder per piece

    Name each folder after the piece. Add a line or two about it: what it's made of, and the story behind it.

  3. III

    Tell us about you

    Your name or studio, where you work, your craft, and a portrait if you'd like one. We'll hang the rest.
